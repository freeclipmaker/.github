### What it does

- **Finds the best moments.** Transcribes your podcast, stream, interview or lesson and picks the most clip-worthy segments.
- **Reframes to 9:16.** Tracks the speaker's face so vertical clips stay centred.
- **Animated captions.** Bold, word-by-word subtitle styles made for short-form video.
- **Copyright-free music.** A built-in library of CC0 tracks you can lay under any clip ([browse the music](https://freeclipmaker.com/music)).
- **Private by design.** Everything runs locally in your browser using WebAssembly and WebGPU, so your video never leaves your device.

### How it works

1. Open [freeclipmaker.com](https://freeclipmaker.com) and sign in with Google.
2. Drop in a long video.
3. Download your clips, ready for Shorts, TikTok and Reels.
